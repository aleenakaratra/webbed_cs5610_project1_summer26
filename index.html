About

I grew up in Kerala, studied computer science at Rajagiri School of Engineering and Technology, and spent a year at Alstom in Hyderabad before starting my master's at Northeastern. This site is modelled after the stops in a rail line to reflect my time at Alstom. There, I worked on the FBANE module, refactoring a multi-component C++/C# codebase used in rail traffic management and helped commission software for the NCRTC and Kiwi systems.

At Northeastern I'm working through the systems-heavy parts of the curriculum which include web development this term, and I hope to do a networking course in the next semester after my co-op. I've done coursework in machine learning (a team project on diabetic retinopathy grading) and cloud architecture (AWS Academy certification in progress).

I like building things that have to actually work, in domains where failure has consequences. Rail signalling is one. Submarine tactical software is the next.
